**Keep the 90-minute slot, but turn it into a short decision meeting where people leave once their item is done.**

🧩 The core conflict: the meeting must exist, but a meeting everyone sits through in full is boring.

### 📋 At a glance

| Idea | One-line summary | Start |
|---|---|---|
| ① Pre-read and leave early | Updates in writing, talk only about decisions | 🟢 |
| ② Rotating host | Each week one person designs the format | 🟡 |
| ③ Two-tier meeting | 30 minutes required, the rest optional | 🟡 |
| 🎲 Wildcard: Reverse order | Start with the conclusions, argue only on objection | 🔴 |

---

### ① Pre-read and leave early 🟢
- 🛠️ What to do: Everyone posts a three-line update the day before. In the meeting, list only the items that need a decision, give each a time box, and let people leave when their items are done.
- 🧪 First experiment: The organizer tries it for 2 weeks. If the meeting ends 30 minutes or more early in at least 1 of 2 weeks, continue.
